Tiny gaps and scraps, under 100 ms: usually left where a swipe stopped just short of a section, or just past one. For each, pick the take that fills it (or silence, for a scrap), or leave it. Apply makes your picks as one undo step; ones you leave stop being counted.
Space or ▶ Play plays and stops. Drag across a take to use it there. Drag a lit section's edge, or a seam in the comp, to move it. Fill gaps from fills the comp's silent gaps with one take. Tidy lists tiny gaps and scraps left by swipes. Snap uses LUNA's own grid. Click a take to switch the comp section under the pointer to it. Swipe across Silence (under the comp) to erase that stretch, or click it to erase the section above; Option-drag on a take does the same. Click a take's name to hear it alone, and Comp to hear the comp. Double-click a take's name to rename it. Comping a group, the grouped tracks show below this track's takes: click one's name to show or hide its takes (swipe on them too), double-click it to work on that track. Click the ruler to move the playhead. LUNA's markers show above the bar numbers; click one to go there. Cmd+scroll zooms, sideways scroll moves along.
